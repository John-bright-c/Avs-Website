Our Story

From a Small Local Team to Bengaluru's Trusted Security Partner

AV Solutions began in Bengaluru with a simple goal: help homes and businesses feel genuinely safe. What started as a small team installing CCTV cameras for local shops has grown into a full-service security and technology company, serving corporates, hospitals, hotels, educational institutions and residences across the city.

Over 12+ years, we've expanded from surveillance into fire safety, biometric access, networking, electrical work and structured cabling — always guided by the same principle: install it right, support it always.

  • 100+ projects completed across sectors
  • In-house certified technical team
  • Long-term AMC partnerships with repeat clients
